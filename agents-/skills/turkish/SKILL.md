# Turkish

Use this skill for Turkish writing, editing, translation, terminology, and tone.

- Prefer natural contemporary Turkish over literal English transfer.
- Keep established technical terms in English; otherwise use clear Turkish.
- Match the requested formality; default to plain, concise, respectful Turkish.
- Avoid inflated, bureaucratic, or unnecessarily archaic wording.
- Prefer `hata düzeltmesi` over `hata giderimi`.
